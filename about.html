Who We Are

CleanPro is a professional cleaning company dedicated to providing high-quality cleaning services for businesses across Slovakia. We understand that a clean workspace is essential for productivity, employee well-being, and making a positive impression on clients and visitors.

Our team consists of experienced cleaning professionals who take pride in their work. We use modern cleaning equipment and eco-friendly products to ensure the best results while caring for the environment.
